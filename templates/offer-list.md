## 3 quick questions

1. Which offer do people buy first, most often?

2. After they buy that, what do they ask you for next? Use their words.

3. How many hours a week can you give to delivery?

## What the 3 delivery types mean

- **They do it:** a guide, a template, a course, a tool. Your time isn't in each sale.
- **With me:** a call, a workshop, coaching, a review. Some of your time is in each sale.
- **For them:** you or your team do the work and hand it over. A lot of your time is in each sale.
