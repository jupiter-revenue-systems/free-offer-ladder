# The Offer Ladder Map

Your AI asks about your offers and maps them low, mid and high, then shows the missing step and the price that fits it.

## The promise

When you finish, you'll know what to sell next and for how much, on one page, with the math shown.

## Who it's for

- Coaches, creators and agencies who don't know what to sell next.
- Anyone with one small offer and one big one, and nothing in between.
- Anyone with only a big offer and no small way for a stranger to say yes.

## What's inside

- `START_HERE.md`: the instructions your AI reads first. It asks about your offers, one question at a time, then draws the map.
- `templates/offer-list.md`: the list to fill in, one line per offer.
- `templates/ladder-map.md`: the one-page map layout.
- `templates/price-fit.md`: the price math and the hours check.

## How to use it

1. Download this pack and unzip it.
2. Fill in `templates/offer-list.md` with everything you sell today.
3. Open a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT and add the whole folder.
4. Type: `Read START_HERE.md and follow it.`
5. Answer the questions. Save the map.
6. Send the one-sentence offer to 5 past buyers before you build anything.

## What it doesn't do

- It maps and prices the missing step. It doesn't build the offer, the page or the checkout.
- The price is a starting point from your own numbers. Real buyers confirm it.
- It doesn't tell you who's buying or what to say.
